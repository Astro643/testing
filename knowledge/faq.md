# Example knowledge base (replace this with your real content)

## How do I verify my account?
Go to the #verify channel and press the green Verify button. If the button does nothing, make sure your Discord account has a verified email address, then try again.

## How do I change my nickname?
Right-click your name in the member list, choose Edit Server Profile, and set a new nickname. Nicknames must follow the rules in #rules.

## How do I report a user?
Use the Report button on the user's message, or open a ticket and include the message link and a short description of what happened.
